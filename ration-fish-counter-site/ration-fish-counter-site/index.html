THE CHALLENGE

When the real fish number is uncertain, decisions get weaker

Accurate counts of juveniles in land‑based tanks are the foundation for correct feeding, density control, and water‑quality management. If the true number of fish is unknown, feeding can overshoot (waste + extra load on the system) or become overly cautious (slower growth).

Transfers between tanks create a unique opportunity to observe each fish — but this is also when fish can move quickly and in dense flow. That makes counting one of the hardest computer‑vision tasks: reliably detecting and tracking individuals without double counting or missing fish.

Designed for smolt handling realities

During handling, fish overlap, speed varies, and lighting can change. Ration counters are built around stability and verification — so the numbers are practical and trustworthy for daily operations.

Value beyond the number

Counting is only the start. Size distribution and biomass estimates help optimize feeding, plan density, and standardize reporting across sites.

SOLUTION

Dry Fish Counter for precise counting + biomass reporting

The Ration Dry Fish Counter is built for counting during grading and transfers, when fish pass individually through a controlled channel outside of water.

Track each fish to avoid double counts

The system observes and analyses fish in real time and tracks individuals between frames. This improves robustness in dense flow compared to simple frame‑by‑frame counting.

Biomass and size distribution

Get a session summary with total count, throughput, and size distribution for better biomass estimates. Export results for reporting and production planning.

FISH WELFARE

Fish welfare by design

Fish welfare is a core consideration in the design of the Ration Dry Fish Counter. The system is engineered to promote smooth handling and reduce stress.

  • Smooth, continuous fish flow with minimal obstruction
  • Short handling time
  • Stable operation without sudden starts and stops
  • Consistent passage geometry to reduce physical contact
